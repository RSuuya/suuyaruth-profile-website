About Suuya Ruth

A vibrant and talented individual from the Basoga ethnic group in Uganda specefically Iganga District. Headed by the Kyabazinga HIS Royal highness Gabula Nadiope The Basoga people are known for their rich cultural heritage, traditions, and contributions to the Ugandan society..

Personal Background

I was born and raised in the Busoga region of Uganda specifically iganga district. Growing up in a close-knit family, i learned the importance of community and cultural values from an early age. my parents instilled in me a strong sense of identity and pride in myu heritage.

As a Musoga, i embrace my cultural traditions, including music, dance, and storytelling. i actively participated in local cultural events and festivals, showcasing the beauty of Musoga culture to both locals and visitors.

Interests

I am passionate about various activities that promote her culture and community:

  • Cultural Dance and Music
  • Community Development Projects
  • Traditional Cooking
  • Environmental Conservation
  • Literature and Creative Writing

Achievements

I have been recognized for efforts in:

  • Promoting local arts and crafts through workshops and exhibitions.
  • Organizing cultural festivals that celebrate Musoga heritage.
  • Advocating for women's rights and empowerment in my community.
  • Participating in environmental conservation initiatives, including tree planting campaigns.
